        Who can open it

          Bring a board from Miro

          1. Open the board in Miro and press ⌘A, then ⌘C.
          2. Click in the box below and press ⌘V.
          3. Give it a name, and press Bring it across.

          Your boards live on your own server and are never uploaded anywhere else. A board opened from here can be edited, and every change is pushed to your server as it is made.
